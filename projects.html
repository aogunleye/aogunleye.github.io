RegiBERT: Continuous Modeling of French Linguistic Registers via Label Distribution Learning, Linear Probing and UMAP Projection

The detection of linguistic registers has historically suffered from rigid classification approaches (hard labels). However, natural language is inherently nuanced and ambiguous. This project introduces RegiBERT, a CamemBERT-based model designed to capture this linguistic continuity. Rather than assigning a single exclusive class to a sentence, the model learned to predict a probability distribution (soft labels) using the annotated TrémoLo corpus. RegiBERT achieves a validation KL divergence loss of 0.365, a MAE of 0.179, and an overall accuracy of 84.5%. Layer-wise probing further reveals that stylistic nuance crystallizes in deeper transformer representations, peaking at layer 12 (R² = 0.556). A 3D UMAP projection calibrated on the validation subdataset confirms this structure geometrically, revealing a continuous manifold where the standard register smoothly connects the formal and colloquial clusters.

Empirical Benchmark: Johnson-Lindenstrauss Lemma on Text Data

Practical benchmark of dimensionality reduction techniques on text data (20newsgroups). Compares PCA against Random Projections, proving that Random Projections preserve pairwise distances while running much faster than standard SVD in high dimensions.

L'Exil Scolaire : Overseas Students' Journey (INSEE Dataviz 2025)

Finalist project (3rd / 17) for the national INSEE Dataviz Competition. Conducted an in-depth statistical investigation into educational mobility inequalities and structural gaps facing students moving from French Overseas Territories to Mainland France. Developed custom visual flows and spatial representations to highlight regional disparities in higher education access.
